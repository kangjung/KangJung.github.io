ZigZag RABBIT

Arcade game: A game where the rabbit moves as far as possible in a zigzag pattern without falling.

Unity

About

Do not fall—zigzag and try to move as far as possible! Just tap the screen to change the rabbit's direction. Try not to fall off the edges and see how far you can go. Simple controls make it easy for anyone to enjoy this casual game.
