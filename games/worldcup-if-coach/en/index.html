About

An interactive World Cup manager simulation built with vibe coding for a hackathon. Choose a 2026 World Cup team, select the lineup, formation, and attacking and defensive tactics, then influence the match through substitutions and tactical changes. After the final whistle, review a manager report based on your decisions.

Player ratings and match results use custom simulation data for the experience and are not real-world predictions.

Highlights

  • Choose from 48 teams and their national-team players
  • Drag-and-drop tactical board and formation setup
  • Tactics-driven match simulation with live manager interventions
  • Post-match manager report and highlights
