About

My Travel Pins is a private travel tracker for marking every country and city you have visited on your own world map.

Color visited countries and regions, group places into trips, and draw travel routes on the map. Review country, city, and continent statistics, add your map to a home screen widget, and share it as an image.

The app works offline with no account or sign-in, and all travel data stays on your device. JSON backup and CSV import/export are also supported.
