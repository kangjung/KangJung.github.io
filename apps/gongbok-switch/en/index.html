About

Fasting Switch helps you manage fasting time, daily missions, body records, and weekly trends in one place.

Set a fasting goal, check progress and remaining time, choose only the routines you need, and review changes in weight and muscle mass by week. Home screen widgets and share cards make it easier to keep your daily rhythm going.

Highlights

  • Fasting timer and goal time tracking
  • Daily routine checklist
  • Daily mood, tags, and memo records
  • Calendar-based record review
  • Weekly stats for average fasting, routine completion, and weight changes
  • Weight and muscle mass trend records
  • Home screen widget
  • Shareable record card
