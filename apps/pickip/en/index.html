01START SMALL

A little at a time.
Not all at once.

A large library does not need a large commitment. Choose a small batch and make one decision at a time. Twenty is a session size, not a daily limit.

  1. 01

    Choose your batch

    Review 20, 50 or 100 photos. Start with your whole library, screenshots, this month’s photos or photos more than a year old.

    2050100
  2. 02

    Swipe, then decide

    Left marks a photo for deletion; right keeps it. Undo your previous choice when needed. Swiping alone does not delete a photo.

  3. 03

    Take one last look

    See your delete candidates together. Keep anything you change your mind about, then choose trash or permanent deletion.

02COMPARE SIMILAR SHOTS

Similar moments.
Keep your favorites.

Compare similar-looking photos taken close together. Take a closer look and choose one or more to keep. The choice stays yours.

03YOU MAKE THE FINAL CALL

A choice,
not a deletion.

Room for a second thought. Picking delete candidates and actually deleting them are separate steps.

  • Review before deleting

    Check your candidates on the final review screen. Tap a photo to keep it instead.

  • Trash or permanent deletion

    Trash is the default. Permanent deletion is a separate choice, and PicKip cannot undo it.

  • A record of your cleanup

    See photos reviewed, photos cleaned and their handled size in your last 30 cleanup sessions.

04PROCESSED ON YOUR DEVICE

Your photos stay local.
Your choices stay yours.

No signup or photo uploads. Photo comparison happens on your device, where your keep choices and cleanup records are stored.

Does swiping left delete a photo immediately?

No. It only marks a candidate for deletion. Nothing is deleted until you make the deletion decision on the final review screen.

Will kept photos appear again?

Not in regular cleanup. Use “Review kept photos again” in Settings to reset your keep history. Similar-photo comparison may show a kept photo as a reference alongside new photos.

Why does the app need photo access?

It reads photo lists, capture times and thumbnails to show photos for cleanup and compare similar shots. With Android’s selected-photo access, only the photos you allow are available.

Does moving photos to trash free space immediately?

Not necessarily. Restore photos or empty the trash in your device gallery. Trash support and retention depend on your device and gallery app.

Is PicKip available on iPhone?

PicKip is currently Android-only. The app supports Korean, English and Japanese; choose your language in Settings.
