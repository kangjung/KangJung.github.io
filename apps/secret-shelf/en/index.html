About

Secret Shelf is a personal reading log app for quietly collecting the books you have read and the sentences that stayed with you.

Record book titles, reading dates, ratings, thoughts, short reviews, and review links. Browse your library as covers or book spines, then look back on your reading flow through monthly reading postcards and yearly statistics.

Build your own calm bookshelf, one book at a time.

Highlights

  • Reading log for completed books
  • Ratings, notes, short reviews, and review locations
  • Cover view and bookshelf spine view
  • Monthly reading postcard
  • Yearly reading statistics
  • No sign-up required
