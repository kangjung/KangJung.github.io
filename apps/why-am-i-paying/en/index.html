About

Subly is a lightweight subscription and recurring expense tracker built for quick manual entry.

Add your subscriptions in seconds, check upcoming payment dates, view monthly and yearly spending, and see how much you have paid over time. Subly does not connect to your bank, card, or any external server. Your data stays on your device.

Add subscriptions quickly with category, amount, billing cycle, and billing date Track monthly and yearly subscriptions View upcoming payments with D-day labels Check spending by category with simple charts Browse payment dates in a calendar Set local reminders before billing dates Edit start dates for more accurate cumulative statistics Export and import backup files manually Dark mode support Subly is designed for people who want a simple way to answer one question: “Why am I paying for this?”
