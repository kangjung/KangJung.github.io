About

Reptile Note is a care record app for reptile and amphibian keepers.

Quickly log feeding, misting, temperature, humidity, weight, cleaning, shedding, egg laying, medication, and vet records, then review everything by date through calendar and timeline views.

You can organize records by animal and enclosure, find specific logs with detailed filters, and use statistics to understand recent activity, feeding intervals, weight changes, and care patterns.

Keep small care details organized so it is easier to notice how your reptiles and amphibians are doing.

Highlights

  • Animal and enclosure management
  • Feeding, misting, temperature, humidity, weight, and care logs
  • Calendar and timeline views
  • Detailed filters by record type, animal, and enclosure
  • Recent activity and record statistics
  • Shareable animal cards
